About us

A national intercollegiate community for women exploring consulting.

Who we are

Girls Who Consult is a national intercollegiate community on a mission to empower the next generation of women consultants through mentorship, education, and hands-on industry experience.


Whether you are curious about consulting or already recruiting, you will find people to learn from and learn with.

What we believe

Mission-driven

Consulting skills are most useful when pointed at problems that matter.

Community first

Women learn faster when they learn together.

Hands-on

Real cases and real mentors beat theory alone.
